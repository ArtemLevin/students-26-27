# LEVIN / ATLAS — Composition Seeds

A seed is a composition grammar, not a skin.

| Seed | Character | Dominant structure | Good fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| CARTOGRAPHER | atlas / map | radial, margins, coordinates | broad curriculum overview |
| LAB | scientific instrument | dense grid, readings, mono labels | diagnostics, active practice |
| EDITORIAL | intellectual journal | large type, asymmetric columns | narrative lesson history |
| BLUEPRINT | engineering drawing | orthogonal grid, dimensions, rules | algebra/geometry, technical learners |
| ARCHIVE | catalog / library | index spine, records, chronology | material-heavy students |
| COSMOS | orbit / constellation | radial hierarchy, sparse field | map-first dashboards |

## A / CARTOGRAPHER
Use the radial map as the visual center. Surround it with coordinates, route-like section indexes and generous paper space. Avoid literal antique-map decoration.

## B / LAB
Use tight measurements, timestamps, percentages and small mono metadata. Interaction should feel precise and immediate. Avoid gamer/neon aesthetics.

## C / EDITORIAL
Let typography create the layout. Use deliberate asymmetry, wide margins and occasional marginal notes. Avoid stacking many boxed panels.

## D / BLUEPRINT
Use orthogonal alignment, hairline grids, dimension-like separators and exact codes. Corners stay near-square. Great for a technical EGE dashboard.

## E / ARCHIVE
Prioritize chronological and categorical indexing. Materials behave like records. Use a visible catalog spine or numbering system.

## F / COSMOS
Use the radial data model as orbital hierarchy. Keep the palette restrained and academic; no starfield wallpaper, neon or sci-fi chrome.

## Living-atlas layer

Route progress, coordinate waypoints, the Navigator symbol, micro-parallax and cross-highlight are cross-cutting interaction primitives rather than a seventh composition seed.

They may be used with CARTOGRAPHER, EDITORIAL, BLUEPRINT, LAB or other seeds when they support the learning model. Avoid adding all primitives automatically.

Darya's reference implementation combines CARTOGRAPHER navigator structure with EDITORIAL lesson structure. Other students should preserve the family grammar while changing at least three expression axes.

Shared route/waypoint/parallax behavior: `shared/student-dashboard/atlas-motion.js`.

## Diversity rule

Two consecutive student sites should differ in at least three fingerprint axes: composition, accent, density, typography character, geometry or motion.
